PROVIYAA — MASTER OPERATING MODEL
1. PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT
This document defines the complete operating story of Proviyaa.
It describes:
* Who exists inside the ecosystem
* What each role is responsible for
* How people enter and move through the organization
* How a lead becomes a customer
* How a customer becomes an active organization
* How requirements are collected
* How the product is demonstrated
* How configuration and implementation happen
* How operations take over
* How support issues are handled
* How product/development teams respond to operational feedback
* How customers continuously provide feedback
* How sales, customer success, operations, support, product, finance and management remain connected
* How merchants operate their own organizations
* How restaurant, store and service businesses use the platform
* How customers interact with OneLatur
* How fulfilment and delivery connect to the merchant ecosystem
* How information flows from one role to another
This document should eventually become the business and operational source of truth from which the system's phased functional requirements can be defined.
This document intentionally does not define the technical implementation.
________________


2. THE PROVIYAA ECOSYSTEM
Proviyaa should be understood as a connected ecosystem rather than a collection of independent dashboards.
At the highest level there are six interconnected operating layers:
Layer 1 — Platform
The people responsible for the entire Proviyaa ecosystem.
Layer 2 — Growth
The people responsible for finding, converting and onboarding businesses.
Layer 3 — Customer Success & Operations
The people responsible for turning a sold customer into a successful, functioning customer.
Layer 4 — Merchant Organization
The business that actually uses Proviyaa.
This may be:
* Restaurant
* Store
* Service business
Layer 5 — Customer / Marketplace
The end consumer interacting with the merchant ecosystem through OneLatur and related customer experiences.
Layer 6 — Fulfilment
The people who physically or operationally fulfil orders, pickups and deliveries.
________________


3. THE CENTRAL OPERATING PRINCIPLE
Every customer should have a continuous journey through Proviyaa.
The journey does not end when Sales closes the deal.
The journey is:
Lead → Conversation → Qualification → Proposal → Payment → Onboarding → Requirement Collection → Configuration → Demo → Training → Go-Live → Daily Operations → Support → Feedback → Improvement → Renewal / Expansion
Therefore, every department is effectively receiving an output from the previous department and creating an output for the next department.
For example:
Sales
creates:
Customer + commercial commitment + initial business understanding
which becomes the input for:
Administration / Onboarding
which creates:
Customer account + business structure + implementation requirements
which becomes the input for:
Customer Success
which creates:
Demonstrated and trained customer
which becomes the input for:
Operations
which creates:
Functioning customer environment
which creates ongoing information for:
Support
which identifies:
Problems, questions and operational friction
which produces information for:
Product / Development
which creates:
Fixes, improvements and product changes
which returns value to:
Customer Success + Operations + Customer
This creates a continuous operating loop.

49. DAILY FEEDBACK
The critical requirement is that feedback does not disappear after the ticket is closed.
Customer Success should be able to say:
"The customer reported this problem on Monday."
Operations should be able to say:
"We investigated it Tuesday."
Product should be able to say:
"We identified the underlying issue Wednesday."
Development should be able to say:
"The change was completed Thursday."
Customer Success should then be able to say:
"We demonstrated the change to the customer Friday."
Customer should be able to confirm:
"Yes, this now works."
This creates a complete feedback chain.
________________
