The city is already moving.
A shop owner is opening shutters.

Proviyaa is not the story of a screen.

Every technical capability must eventually answer:
Who?
A real person.
Where?
A real operating situation.
What are they trying to do?
A real task.
What do they need?
Information, decision, action or handoff.
Who receives the work next?
Another person or an automated process.
What happens if something goes wrong?
The work moves to an exception path.
What does the business learn?
Every meaningful transaction creates knowledge.
What happens next?
